A diary, not a diagnosis

Back on the Menu helps you plan and record a low-FODMAP reintroduction. It is not a medical device. It doesn’t diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition, and it doesn’t replace advice from your doctor or dietitian.

  • The portions in the app are common starting amounts. If your dietitian gave you different ones, use theirs; you can change them for each test.
  • Results describe how you felt during each test. They aren’t a diagnosis. Share them with your doctor or dietitian.
  • See a doctor first, and don’t self-test, if you have blood in your stool, unexplained weight loss, fever, symptoms that wake you at night, or symptoms that started after age 50. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Free and Pro

Free: your first food test, the elimination tracker, the diary, the picture of your profile, backups and the CSV export. Pro adds every further food test and the PDF report.
