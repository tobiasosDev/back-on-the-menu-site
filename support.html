Common questions

When can I start testing foods?

When your symptoms have settled on the low-FODMAP diet and your dietitian or doctor agrees. Until then, the Today screen counts your elimination days.

Can I use my dietitian’s portions?

Yes. Before you start a test, you can change the test food and each of the three portions. The amounts in the app are common starting points.

Something else could explain a bad day

A cold, your period, stress or a restaurant meal can bring symptoms too. Write it in the check-in note, so it shows up in your diary and in the report. If you’re unsure about a result, talk to your dietitian and test that group again later.

I forgot to check in

Open the Diary and tap Add a past day. Symptoms can show up overnight, so the next morning’s check-in also counts for the portion you ate the day before.

How do I share my results with my dietitian?

Open Your profile and tap Make a report for my doctor to create a one-page PDF (Pro), or Share my results for a picture of your profile. The full diary is in the Diary under Export CSV.

How do I move my diary to a new iPhone?

On the old iPhone, open Settings in the app and tap Download a backup, then save the file to Files or iCloud Drive. On the new iPhone, tap Restore a backup. Your iPhone’s own backup through iCloud also includes Back on the Menu.

I bought Pro. How do I get it back?

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Back on the Menu Pro. If you cancel during the free week, you aren’t charged. The app reminds you two days before the free week ends.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. We hold no copy, so there is nothing to delete on our side. Backup files you saved yourself stay where you put them.
